SOFTWARE AND METHODS REFERENCES

This document lists the software, libraries, and databases used in the UFM1 Splicing Functional Impact Pipeline, along with their versions and primary scholarly references.

================================================================================
CORE BIOINFORMATICS SOFTWARE
================================================================================

rMATS (v4.1.2)
- Purpose: Differential splicing quantification
- Reference: Shen et al. (2014). rMATS: robust and flexible detection of differential alternative splicing from replicate RNA-Seq data. PNAS. [https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1419161111]

MaxEntScan (Python Port)
- Purpose: Splice site strength scoring
- Reference: Yeo and Burge (2004). Maximum entropy modeling of short sequence motifs with applications to RNA splicing signals. J Comput Biol. [https://doi.org/10.1089/1066527041424143]

MEME Suite (AME v5.x)
- Purpose: Analysis of Motif Enrichment
- Reference: McLeay and Bailey (2010). Motif enrichment analysis: a unified framework and an evaluation on ChIP data. Bioinformatics. [https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btq248]

SignalP (v6.0)
- Purpose: Signal peptide prediction
- Reference: Teufel et al. (2022). SignalP 6.0: predicting signal peptides using deep neural networks. Nat Methods. [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-021-01356-w]

clusterProfiler (v>=4.0)
- Purpose: Gene Ontology enrichment analysis
- Reference: Wu et al. (2021). clusterProfiler 4.0: A universal enrichment tool for interpreting omics data. The Innovation. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xinn.2021.100141]

BEDTools
- Purpose: Genomic interval arithmetic
- Reference: Quinlan and Hall (2010). BEDTools: a flexible suite of utilities for comparing genomic features. Bioinformatics. [https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btq033]

SAMtools
- Purpose: Sequence alignment manipulation
- Reference: Danecek et al. (2021). Twelve years of SAMtools and BCFtools. GigaScience. [https://doi.org/10.1093/gigascience/giab008]

- R (v>=4.3): Statistical analysis and visualization.
